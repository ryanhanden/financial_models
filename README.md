# Financial Modeling & Commercial Real Estate Underwriting Portfolio

A collection of self-directed commercial real estate underwriting, investment analysis, and corporate financial modeling projects.

The portfolio is designed to demonstrate practical modeling ability across property-level underwriting, leveraged returns, valuation, debt structures, sensitivity analysis, and integrated financial statements.

---

## Commercial Real Estate Underwriting

### Briarwood Apartments: Livermore, CA

**File:** `Briarwood_Apartments_Livermore_Underwriting.xlsx`

Multifamily acquisition underwriting for a 64-unit Class B apartment property acquired at a modeled purchase price of **$19.75 million**.

The model includes:

* Unit-level rent roll and revenue assumptions
* 10-year operating pro forma
* Vacancy, other income, and operating expense projections
* Acquisition financing and amortization schedule
* 67% LTV senior loan at 5.58% fixed interest
* Two-year interest-only period followed by 30-year amortization
* Levered and unlevered cash flows
* Year-10 exit valuation
* IRR, equity multiple, and cash-on-cash returns
* Exit-cap, rent-growth, and purchase-price sensitivity analysis

### Key Underwriting Outputs

* Going-in cap rate: **5.85%**
* Exit cap rate: **6.75%**
* Levered IRR: **10.0%**
* Equity multiple: **2.3x**
* Average cash-on-cash return: **6.2%**

The project was built to practice the fundamental acquisition underwriting process: translating a rent roll and operating assumptions into NOI, sizing debt, projecting equity cash flows, and evaluating risk across multiple exit scenarios.

### Parkview Apartments: Sacramento, CA

**File:** `Parkview_Apartments_ValueAdd_Waterfall.xlsx`

Value-add multifamily acquisition and renovation underwriting for a **48-unit apartment property** acquired at a modeled purchase price of **$8.4 million**.

The business plan assumes approximately **$18,000 per unit of renovation spending**, phased renovations, rent increases following unit turns, bridge financing, and a five-year hold.

The model includes:

* Existing and renovated rent assumptions
* Phased renovation schedule
* Renovation costs and unit-turn timing
* Revenue ramp through stabilization
* Operating expense projections
* Interest-only bridge financing
* Five-year leveraged cash flow
* Stabilized NOI and yield-on-cost analysis
* Exit valuation
* Levered IRR and equity multiple
* LP/GP partnership waterfall
* 8% preferred return
* Return of capital
* 70/30 promote structure

### Key Underwriting Outputs

* Stabilized yield on cost: **7.6%**
* Exit cap rate: **6.5%**
* Deal-level levered IRR: **18.6%**
* Deal-level equity multiple: **2.2x**
* LP IRR: **16.6%**
* LP equity multiple: **2.0x**
* GP IRR: **32.3%**
* GP promote: approximately **$530,000**

The project was built to understand how value-add execution translates into property-level returns and how those returns are ultimately distributed between the LP and GP through a promote structure.

## Corporate Finance & Valuation

### Airbnb, Inc.: Three-Statement Model and DCF

**File:** `Airbnb_Model.xlsx`

Fully integrated three-statement operating model and unlevered discounted cash flow valuation built from SEC filings.

The model includes:

* Historical financial statements
* Five-year operating forecast
* Driver-based revenue and expense assumptions
* Working-capital schedules
* Integrated income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow statement
* CAPM-based cost of equity
* WACC calculation
* Unlevered free cash flow
* Gordon-growth terminal value
* Enterprise-to-equity bridge
* Two-variable valuation sensitivity analysis

The model is structured so operating assumptions flow through the three statements and ultimately into valuation.
