## How to read the models

- Blue cells are inputs, black cells are formulas, and green cells link to other tabs.
- Most models start with a Summary tab and keep every assumption, with its source, on an Inputs or Assumptions tab.
- Share prices and market data are as of the dates noted in each file. These are research exercises, not investment advice.
